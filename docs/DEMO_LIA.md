# Legitimate interests assessment — demonstration access

This is an internal operational assessment. It is not external legal advice and it is not a certification.

## Purpose

MEEM processes limited professional contact information so a person who has requested demonstration access can receive, verify and use that demonstration and so MEEM can respond regarding that request.

## Necessity

The request cannot practically be administered without identifying the requester and communicating with the email address they supplied.

## Balancing

- The person initiated the request.
- Only limited information is collected: name, email, organisation, role, and optional evaluation interest.
- Processing is directly connected to the person's request.
- No unrelated marketing enrolment occurs.
- No special-category information is requested.
- Access tokens contain no personal information.
- Demo access expires.
- The person can contact MEEM and object to the processing.

## Conclusion

Legitimate interests is considered proportionate for administration and direct follow-up concerning the requested demonstration.

A new HubSpot contact is recorded with the internal value `Legitimate interest – prospect/lead`. The en dash is part of that value. An existing contact keeps the lawful basis already stored.
